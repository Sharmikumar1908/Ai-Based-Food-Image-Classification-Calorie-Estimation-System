PROJECT NOTES / METHOD & RESPONSIBILITY

About this prototype

A small, local-first computer-vision demonstration for learning and evaluation.

PURPOSE

Describe what is visible.
Do not infer what is not.

This project detects face regions in classroom media and, when a locally trained CNN is available, classifies each detected crop into four approximate visual categories: facing forward, looking away, head down, or unclear.

These labels describe visible image cues only. They are not a measurement of concentration, emotion, effort, comprehension, participation, or learning, and they should not be used to evaluate or discipline an individual.

Method

  1. AcquireRead an uploaded image/video or one webcam still in the browser.
  2. DetectUse OpenCV's bundled Haar cascades for frontal/profile face regions.
  3. ClassifyUse an optional trained compact CNN; without it, label detections Unclear.
  4. SummarizeShow observation counts and store aggregate session metadata in SQLite.
LIMITATIONS
  • Camera angle, lighting, distance, motion blur, and occlusion affect detection.
  • Haar cascades may miss small, turned, or partially visible faces.
  • A CNN is only as representative and well-labeled as its training data.
  • Different people may express or hold a pose differently.
  • Video totals count frame observations and can count the same person repeatedly.
Privacy by design

Media is processed on the local machine, temporary video files are deleted, and history stores no images, names, or biometric identifiers.
